1Mill SupplyAugust 22, 2026

Howe Sound Idle Continues to Reshape the B.C. Pulp-Supply Picture

CityNews reported on the regional impact of Domtar's announcement that Howe Sound operations will be idled indefinitely, affecting the mill workforce and local supply chain. The report follows the company's statement about demand, pricing and fibre challenges.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: A supply disruption can reach converters through replacement grades, longer routes or changed roll properties. Keep an approved-substitute process ready and use a first-article trial before changing routine production.

Source: CityNews report on the Howe Sound mill

2EU Packaging RulesAugust 22, 2026

EU Packaging Rules Put More Pressure on Exporters to Prove Material Choices

Vietnam News reported that the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation is in its general application phase and is pushing exporters to rethink packaging materials, design and recycling. The report also describes opportunities around recycled plastics, testing, certification and collection.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Export-oriented converters should align the roll specification with the customer's compliance file: layer structure, recycled content, material declaration, lot code, test method and finished-roll inspection.

Source: Vietnam News on EU packaging rules

3Paper and Tissue EventAugust 20, 2026

Paper & Tissue Africa Brings Paper and Hygiene Buyers Together in Nairobi

PaperFIRST lists Paper & Tissue Africa as an August 20–22 exhibition in Nairobi. The event sits alongside the week's nonwoven machinery activity and gives regional suppliers and buyers a forum for paper, tissue and converting discussions.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Regional events can surface new grade and format requirements. Machinery suppliers should collect material samples, target roll drawings and utility constraints before turning an exhibition conversation into an RFQ.

Source: PaperFIRST event listing

4Molded FibreAugust 18, 2026

Cirkla's Georgia Project Adds a Regional Fibre-Packaging Production Signal

Cirkla announced a molded-fibre meat-packaging facility in Gainesville, Georgia, with production planned for the first quarter of 2027. The company says the project will include precision automation, quality laboratories and finishing capability.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Fibre packaging may arrive as sheets, trays or converted formats rather than conventional rolls. Confirm the actual incoming format, trimming method, inspection points and packaging interface before selecting a roll-converting machine.

Source: Cirkla announcement via PR Newswire

5Recycled BoardAugust 17, 2026

MM Board & Paper Uses Arnsberg to Strengthen European Cartonboard Flexibility

MM Board & Paper said its proposed acquisition of the R.D.M. Arnsberg business would add a recycled-cartonboard location and support supply security and production flexibility. The agreement remains subject to approvals and is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Procurement teams should treat a mill transaction as a reason to revalidate roll dimensions, grade codes, cores, moisture and delivery terms before updating a machine recipe or customer approval list.

Source: MM Group press release on Arnsberg

1Absorbent Hygiene EventAugust 22, 2026

EDANA's OUTLOOK 2026 Will Focus on Regulation, Wipes and Circularity

EDANA's OUTLOOK 2026 is scheduled for September 22–24 in Cascais and will cover absorbent hygiene, personal care, wipes, product safety, environmental compliance and circularity. The event reflects the broad set of requirements now shaping hygiene-product development.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: When product and regulatory priorities change together, the converting line must preserve material identity and process records. Include recipe management, inspection, packaging and operator training in the machine scope.

Source: EDANA OUTLOOK 2026 event coverage

2Regional Nonwoven MarketAugust 22, 2026

Nonwoven Tech Africa Closes a Three-Day Machinery and Materials Event

Nonwoven Tech Africa runs from August 20 to 22 in Nairobi and covers hygiene, medical, filtration, construction and other nonwoven applications. Its exhibitor profile includes production lines, converting equipment, finishing systems and raw materials.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: A new market often needs equipment that can handle several roll formats and operator skill levels. Buyers should specify changeover method, spare parts, safety training, local service and the material range expected after launch.

Source: Nonwoven Tech Africa event listing

3Hygiene Raw MaterialsAugust 22, 2026

A Current Hygiene-Materials Overview Points to Continued Regional Growth

A current supplier-market overview described ongoing demand for hygiene raw materials across baby care, feminine care, adult incontinence and home care, with growth attention on emerging markets. The source highlights fluff pulp, SAP and nonwoven materials as parts of the wider supply chain.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Market-growth claims should not replace a customer requirement sheet. Confirm the substrate, parent-roll width, finished-roll format, speed and downstream application before sizing a converting line.

Source: Hygiene raw-materials market overview

4Nonwoven RegulationAugust 21, 2026

Policy Discussions Continue to Emphasise Hygiene and Medical Performance

International Fiber Journal described legislative work around recycling definitions, packaging rules, product restrictions and chemical requirements for nonwoven materials. The analysis stresses that hygiene and medical functionality need to be represented in implementation discussions.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Keep regulatory claims separate from machine claims, but make the line auditable. Lot traceability, controlled recipes, clean handling and finished-roll inspection help customers answer downstream questions.

Source: International Fiber Journal legislative analysis

5Hygiene AdhesivesAugust 20, 2026

Short-Fibre Processing Remains a Material-and-Process Qualification Topic

H.B. Fuller's hygiene materials discussion describes adhesive options intended to support short fibres, lower application temperatures and resource efficiency. The company links adhesive formulation with fibre bonding, core integrity and production-line performance.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: If a short-fibre or adhesive change reaches a nonwoven converting plant, document the material lot and trial conditions. Check web tracking, slitting, winding, storage and downstream product performance before release.

Source: H.B. Fuller INDEX 26 event information

1Packaging DataAugust 21, 2026

New EU Packaging Data Is Raising the Bar for Material Evidence

Packaging Post highlighted new European Commission Joint Research Centre data showing plastic was the only major packaging material to grow between 2011 and the reported comparison period. The article places the data in the context of packaging sustainability and circularity decisions.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Data-driven packaging decisions still need production evidence. Film converters should connect the chosen layer structure and recycled-content claim to roll IDs, test results and the process conditions used to make the finished material.

Source: Packaging Post packaging and recycling coverage

2Flexible CollectionAugust 21, 2026

Mandatory Collection Plans Could Expand the Role of Film-Scrap Control

Valpak modelling reported by Letsrecycle points to a large potential increase in flexible-plastic collection in England when mandatory household collection is introduced. The analysis also underlines the gap between material entering the waste stream and material currently collected.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: A converter preparing for higher recovery expectations should separate clean production trim from mixed waste, label the material stream and keep enough process data to demonstrate what was recovered.

Source: Letsrecycle flexible-plastics collection analysis

3Recycled BOPPJune 9, 2026

Recycled PP BOPP Trials Highlight the Need for Roll-by-Roll Validation

PureCycle and Innovia reported trial production of a white cavitated BOPP film with recycled polypropylene for food, confectionery and label applications. The companies described the trial as a step toward using recycled content in a demanding flexible-film format.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: A recycled-content film should be approved from measured roll data, not from the resin name. Compare slit edge, roll hardness, winding density, print and sealing behaviour with the customer's reference film.

Source: PureCycle and Innovia announcement

4Recycled PP PackagingAugust 17, 2026

Standard Recycled-PP Formats Are Intended to Shorten Brand Qualification

PureCycle and IPL Schoeller introduced SnapPack Square EVO as a standard tamper-evident format containing 20% recycled polypropylene. The companies present the product as a way for brand owners to adopt recycled content without starting a fully custom container programme.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Shorter qualification programmes still require incoming-material inspection, traceability and finished-format checks. The converting line should preserve batch and recipe information through the complete roll or sheet workflow.

Source: Packaging Europe on SnapPack Square EVO

5Mono-Material PEJune 22, 2026

MDO-PE Structures Keep Tension and Winding in the Sustainability Conversation

PlasticsToday reported on a recyclable matte MDO-PE pouch structure developed by Walki and Dow for frozen, dry and fresh food applications. The structure combines an oriented PE print web with a PE-based sealant layer and was designed around low-temperature sealing.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Oriented PE can respond differently to heat, shrinkage and tension than PET/PE laminates. Buyers should require a roll-handling and slitting trial that records curl, edge quality, winding density and downstream sealing behaviour.

Source: PlasticsToday on the Walki and Dow MDO-PE pouch
