Paper, Nonwoven and Film News for July 22, 2026

Fifteen practical stories selected for overseas buyers tracking paper capacity, nonwoven roll-goods demand, wipes and hygiene process changes, recyclable film rules, PCR traceability and roll-material quality control.

HDPTH Daily Market BriefingPaper | Nonwoven | Film

Today's briefing points to capacity and specification decisions across roll materials: paper makers are moving machines toward packaging grades while pulp and energy costs stay in focus; nonwoven producers are adding application-specific lines for airlaid, wipes, automotive and hygiene uses; and film suppliers are working through PCR traceability, raw-material inflation and mono-material formats. 1Packaging Paper CapacityJuly 21, 2026

Palm Converts King's Lynn PM7 to Recycled CCM

Paper Advance reported that Palm will repurpose PM7 at its King's Lynn, UK mill to produce recycled corrugated case material from the first quarter of 2027. The shift turns a major newsprint asset toward packaging grades as European graphic-paper demand keeps declining.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: More recycled containerboard supply can change dust, moisture and edge-strength profiles, so converters should validate slitting cleanliness, tension recipes and roll build before adopting new grades.

Source: Paper Advance on Palm King's Lynn PM7 conversion

2Pulp Sourcing and CostJuly 20, 2026

Finch Paper Ends Pulp Production at Glens Falls

PaperAge reported that Finch Paper will stop producing pulp at its Glens Falls, New York mill and buy pulp on the open market while continuing paper manufacturing. The company said the change lets it focus on paper production after pulp-market economics shifted.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: When mills change pulp sourcing, converters may see grade-to-grade variation in stiffness, dusting and web strength, making incoming roll inspection and adjustable machine settings more important.

Source: PaperAge on Finch Paper Glens Falls pulp decision

3Packaging Demand SignalJuly 20, 2026

U.S. Packaging Papers Shipments Rise 2% in June

AF&PA said total U.S. packaging papers and specialty packaging shipments in June increased 2% from June 2025 and were up 6% for the first six months versus the same period of 2025. The report points to continuing demand for packaging-paper formats.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Rising packaging-paper shipments support investment in slit-to-width, rewinding and roll-handling capacity, especially where customers need shorter lead times and consistent finished-roll quality.

Source: AF&PA June 2026 Packaging Papers Monthly Report

4Energy and Compliance CostJuly 20, 2026

Cepi Warns ETS Changes Could Reduce Paper Decarbonisation Investment

Paper Advance reported that Cepi warned proposed EU Emissions Trading System changes could raise carbon costs and reduce investment capacity for pulp and paper decarbonisation. Energy and carbon policy remain direct cost variables for mills and downstream paper users.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Energy-cost pressure often pushes mills and converters to remove bottlenecks, cut waste and document efficiency gains, strengthening the case for automation, inspection and trim-control upgrades.

Source: Paper Advance on Cepi EU ETS warning

5Tissue Converting InvestmentJuly 19, 2026

Eruslu Adds Six Jumbo-to-Pallet Tissue Converting Lines

Tissue World Magazine reported that Eruslu Hygienic Products is entering tissue production with six Futura and Plusline-supplied Andromeda Tissue converting lines at its Bursa site in Turkiye. The investment is a direct move from hygiene manufacturing into finished tissue production.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Integrated tissue projects show why parent-roll handling, embossing, perforation, rewinding and packaging flow should be specified together rather than treated as separate machinery decisions.

Source: Tissue World Magazine on Eruslu tissue converting investment

1Automotive Nonwovens CapacityJuly 22, 2026

Autoneum Adds Acoustic Nonwoven Capacity in China

Nonwovens News reported that Autoneum is building two new production facilities in Anhui, China to make interior and acoustic components for recently awarded vehicle programmes, including contracts with Chery. The expansion supports a local-for-local supply model for Chinese OEMs.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Automotive acoustic materials are often thick, compressible and specification-sensitive, so converters need robust web guiding, dust control, clean slitting and stable rewinding for technical nonwoven rolls.

Source: Nonwovens News on Autoneum China expansion

2Wipes Regulation and MaterialsJuly 17, 2026

EDANA Outlook 2026 to Examine Plastic-Free Wipes

Nonwovens News reported that EDANA will use its Outlook 2026 conference in Portugal to examine the technical and regulatory implications of moving wet wipes away from plastic. The agenda reflects ongoing pressure on the global wipes supply chain to redesign substrates.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Plastic-free wipes can behave differently during unwinding, perforating, folding and lotion loading, so machine buyers should test low-tension handling and cut quality before committing to scale.

Source: Nonwovens News on EDANA Outlook plastic-free wipes agenda

3Nonwoven Equipment SupplyJuly 17, 2026

Rieter Integrates Barmag and Oerlikon Nonwoven

Nonwovens News reported that Rieter completed the initial integration of Barmag, including Oerlikon Nonwoven, into a new Man-Made Fiber Division. The move broadens Rieter's systems offering from natural fibres into man-made fibres and nonwoven technologies.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Supplier consolidation can affect line availability, service models and upgrade roadmaps, so buyers planning spunbond, meltblown or downstream converting projects should track support coverage and integration timing.

Source: Nonwovens News on Rieter Barmag integration

4Airlaid Absorbent MaterialsJuly 15, 2026

Magic Orders ANDRITZ Airlaid Line for Food Pads

ANDRITZ said Italy-based Magic S.p.A. ordered a food pad eXcelle line with airlaid technology for absorbent food pads, medical devices, personal care and hygiene materials. Commissioning is scheduled for the first quarter of 2027.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Airlaid absorbent structures depend on uniform web formation and retention performance, so downstream converters should control dust, edge trim, rewind density and inspection for pads and hygiene roll goods.

Source: ANDRITZ on Magic S.p.A. food pad line

5Hygiene Core DemandJuly 8, 2026

Absorbent Nonwoven Cores Forecast to Grow Through 2035

Nonwovens News reported that global demand for absorbent nonwoven cores is expected to grow through 2035, driven by ageing populations, hygiene uptake in emerging markets and premium-grade product shifts. The materials are functional intermediates for diapers and hygiene products.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: More absorbent-core demand raises the need for repeatable parent-roll preparation, clean slitting, splice control and quality records across topsheet, core wrap, acquisition and backsheet materials.

Source: Nonwovens News on absorbent nonwoven core growth

1PCR TraceabilityJuly 20, 2026

Amcor Gains RecyClass Certification for PCR Stretch Films

Packaging Insights reported that Amcor received RecyClass recycled-plastic traceability certification for selected Bontite Sustane stretch-film products for pallet wraps. The certification validates defined PCR content and helps customers prepare for UK Plastic Packaging Tax changes.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: PCR traceability is becoming a purchasing requirement, so film converters need lot-level data, stable winding, clean edge profiles and documentation that can survive customer audits.

Source: Packaging Insights on Amcor RecyClass PCR stretch film certification

2Film Raw-Material CostJuly 8, 2026

Flexible Packaging Material Prices Jump in Q2

Flexible Packaging Europe said flexible packaging material markets recorded substantial price increases in the second quarter of 2026, with quarterly minimum prices rising across substrates compared with the previous quarter. The group linked the pressure to geopolitical tensions and raw-material volatility.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: When film costs rise, converters have stronger incentives to reduce trim waste, improve first-pass roll quality and use accurate tension settings to avoid scrapping expensive substrates.

Source: Flexible Packaging Europe on Q2 2026 raw-material prices

3BOPP Market DemandJuly 15, 2026

BOPP Film Demand Approaches 11 Million Tonnes

Converting Quarterly reported AMI analysis showing global BOPP film demand reached nearly 11 million tonnes in 2025 and continued to grow despite disruption from destocking, tariffs and weaker macro conditions. BOPP remains central to snacks, labels and flexible packaging.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Sustained BOPP demand supports investment in high-speed slitting, static control, edge inspection and winding systems that can protect print registration and seal performance.

Source: Converting Quarterly on AMI 2026 BOPP demand analysis

4BOPP Film CapacityJune 8, 2026

Oben Orders 12-Meter BOPP Line for Brazil

Oben Group announced an agreement for a new 12-meter BOPP line in Brazil with 94,000 tons of annual capacity. The project expands regional film availability for flexible packaging and label markets.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: New jumbo-roll film capacity creates downstream demand for precise slitting and rewinding assets that can convert wide BOPP webs into customer-ready rolls without blocking, telescoping or edge defects.

Source: Oben Group on Brazil BOPP line expansion

5Wet Wipes FilmJune 2, 2026

Mondi Wet-Wipes FlowWrap Uses 35% PCR Mono-PP

Mondi launched re/loop FlowWrap, a mono-PP form-fill-and-seal material for wet wipes packaging with 35% post-consumer recycled content. The company said internal tests and customer trials showed performance comparable to virgin alternatives.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Wet-wipes film must seal, run and protect moisture-sensitive packs, so converters should validate COF, gauge variation, static, slit quality and rewind consistency before switching to PCR rollstock.

Source: Mondi on re/loop FlowWrap for wet wipes packaging
