1U.S. Packaging PapersAugust 19, 2026

AF&PA Reports July Packaging-Paper Shipments Up 1 Percent

The American Forest & Paper Association reported that U.S. packaging papers and specialty packaging shipments increased 1% year over year in July and were 5% higher for the first seven months. Unbleached packaging-paper operating rate reached 87.5%, while inventories remained below the year-ago level.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Packaging converters should keep grade, basis-weight and roll-width assumptions explicit in RFQs because packaging-paper availability and printing-paper softness are moving in different directions.

Source: AF&PA July 2026 Packaging Papers Monthly Report

2Recycled CartonboardAugust 17, 2026

MM Board & Paper Agrees to Acquire R.D.M. Arnsberg Mill

MM Board & Paper signed an agreement to acquire the operating business of R.D.M. Arnsberg in Germany through an asset deal. MM describes the site as a recycled-cartonboard mill with roughly 230,000 tonnes of annual capacity, subject to regulatory approval.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: A change in mill ownership can change product mix, service contacts and qualification timing, so European cartonboard buyers should request an updated grade and roll-supply plan before changing slitting schedules.

Source: MM Group press release on the Arnsberg agreement

3Molded Fibre PackagingAugust 18, 2026

Cirkla Plans a U.S. Molded-Fibre Meat-Packaging Facility in Georgia

Cirkla announced a Gainesville, Georgia facility for molded-fibre meat packaging, with production planned to begin in the first quarter of 2027. The company says the site will include automation, quality laboratories and finishing capability for MAP and overwrap trays.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Fibre formats moving from pilot work to regional production create new requirements for sheet handling, trimming, inspection and packaging-line compatibility; buyers should qualify the actual fibre format rather than assume it behaves like paper rollstock.

Source: Cirkla announcement via PR Newswire

4Pharmaceutical PackagingAugust 13, 2026

Faller Packaging Expands in Southeast Europe Through Paperpack

Faller Packaging announced the acquisition of Greek pharmaceutical-packaging specialist Paperpack. The companies serve pharmaceutical and healthcare packaging markets and said the combination strengthens Faller's European reach while the transaction remains subject to customary approvals.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Regulated paperboard packaging places more weight on traceability, clean converting and controlled changeovers. Roll and sheet converters should include inspection records and batch identification in the equipment specification.

Source: Faller Packaging trade press page

5Packaging PolicyAugust 19, 2026

New Packaging Rules Create a Design and Process Opportunity

ThePackagingPortal reported that the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation has entered its application phase, bringing requirements around minimisation, recyclability and recycled content. The practical effect is a stronger need to connect packaging design decisions with material evidence and production records.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: When a structure changes for recyclability, the converting trial should measure web tension, curl, dust, edge quality and finished-roll behaviour instead of approving the new material from a laboratory sample alone.

Source: ThePackagingPortal industry-news archive

1Textile ComplianceAugust 19, 2026

OEKO-TEX Coverage Points to More EU-Rule Preparation for Textile Materials

Ecotextile News highlighted continuing work around OEKO-TEX requirements and the way new European rules are changing material compliance discussions. The development is relevant to nonwoven suppliers that need to keep chemical, product and customer documentation aligned.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: A nonwoven converting line does not create compliance by itself, but it must preserve lot identity and prevent mix-ups when multiple treated or certified webs run through the same slitting and rewinding area.

Source: Ecotextile News archive

2Fibre InvestmentAugust 19, 2026

Spinnova Plans U.S. Listing to Support Material Scale-Up

Ecotextile News reported that Spinnova is planning a U.S. listing as it works to fund the next stage of its fibre platform. The story reflects continued capital-market interest in lower-impact fibre technologies, even while commercial scale-up remains the key execution challenge.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: New fibre grades can arrive with unfamiliar elongation, lint and roll-density behaviour. Converters should run a controlled material trial and keep rewind recipes separate until the grade is stable.

Source: Ecotextile News archive

3Wipes IndustryAugust 18, 2026

INDA Keeps Wipes Development and Regulation on the 2026 Agenda

INDA's World of Wipes programme covers sustainable packaging, substrate evaluation, formulation science, decarbonisation and changing wipes regulations. The programme is a useful signal of the technical issues that wipe manufacturers are prioritising across the supply chain.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Wipe converters should bring substrate, lotion, packaging and end-use requirements into the same trial plan, because a material change can alter winding, sealing, pack-out and downstream dispensing.

Source: INDA World of Wipes programme

4Absorbent Hygiene TestingAugust 18, 2026

BASF Opens a Mumbai Performance Lab for Diapers and SAP

BASF opened a global performance laboratory in Mumbai focused on diapers and superabsorbent polymers. Coverage says the facility supports SAP evaluation, diaper benchmarking and application development for hygiene producers.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: More testing capacity can shorten material-development cycles, but each approved substrate still needs a production trial that checks web tracking, slitting, roll formation and the downstream hygiene process.

Source: TEXtalks report on the BASF Mumbai lab

5Hygiene AdhesivesAugust 20, 2026

H.B. Fuller Highlights Short-Fibre and Lower-Use Adhesive Options

H.B. Fuller's hygiene programme discusses lower-application-temperature adhesives, high-mileage solutions, bio-based options and a core adhesive intended to support short fibres. The company presented the work in the context of performance, sustainability and process efficiency.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: When absorbent-core materials or adhesive add-on changes, converters should recheck web tension, bond integrity, trim cleanliness and finished-roll storage rather than transfer an old machine recipe unchanged.

Source: H.B. Fuller hygiene and nonwovens overview

1Chemical RecyclingAugust 19, 2026

JRC Review Says Chemical Recycling Still Faces Cost Challenges

The Food Packaging Forum reported on a European Commission Joint Research Centre assessment that chemical recycling does not currently achieve cost parity with virgin plastics, while it may still have a role where recycled-content requirements create demand for suitable feedstock.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Film buyers should distinguish a technology claim from a qualified rollstock supply. Ask for contamination limits, recycled-content evidence, film properties and a converting trial before changing a PE or PP specification.

Source: Food Packaging Forum on the JRC chemical-recycling assessment

2PPWR GuidanceAugust 7, 2026

Stretch-Wrap Guidance Raises Questions About When Film Becomes Packaging

The Flexible Packaging Association discussed European Commission FAQ wording that treats stretch wrap sold on a roll for pallet stabilisation as packaging, even when it is later cut for use. The association noted that the interpretation is guidance rather than a legally binding ruling.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Stretch-film producers should map who supplies the finished packaging format, retain product and roll documentation, and make sure slit widths and roll labels match the market and compliance position.

Source: Flexible Packaging Association on PPWR stretch-wrap guidance

3Recycled BOPPJune 9, 2026

PureCycle and Innovia Report a Recycled-Content White BOPP Trial

PureCycle and Innovia announced production and trial evaluation of a white cavitated BOPP film using PureFive recycled polypropylene. The companies positioned the work for snack, confectionery and roll-fed-label applications and reported comparable processing and performance in their testing.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Recycled BOPP still needs line-specific checks for haze, stiffness, sealing, slit edge and winding density. A buyer should request the trial method and the actual film data sheet before treating the result as a production guarantee.

Source: PureCycle and Innovia announcement

4Recycled PP PackagingAugust 17, 2026

PureCycle and IPL Schoeller Launch a Standard Recycled-PP Container

PureCycle and IPL Schoeller introduced SnapPack Square EVO, a tamper-evident container described as containing 20% PureFive recycled polypropylene. The off-the-shelf approach is intended to reduce the time required for brand owners to adopt recycled content.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Standard recycled-content formats can increase demand for repeatable PP roll and sheet supply. Converters should check material identity, lot traceability, sealing or forming behaviour and the accepted inspection method.

Source: Packaging Europe on the PureCycle and IPL container

5Film CollectionAugust 3, 2026

California Commercial-Film Pilots Target Backroom and Warehouse Streams

Waste Dive reported that Flexible Film Recycling Alliance pilots are collecting more film from warehouses, distribution centres, retail backrooms and manufacturing facilities. The pilots are intended to test collection, contamination control and credible end markets.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Clean commercial film streams make trim recovery and roll identification more valuable. Slitter buyers should define how scrap is separated, labelled and transferred so recycling claims are supported by material records.

Source: Waste Dive on commercial film-recycling pilots
