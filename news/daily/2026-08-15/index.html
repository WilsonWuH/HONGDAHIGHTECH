1Tissue Mill InvestmentAugust 13, 2026

Xuong Giang Paper Starts Up TM16 Tissue Line with ANDRITZ Stock Preparation in Vietnam

ANDRITZ reported Xuong Giang Paper successfully commissioned its new TM16 tissue production line at its mill in Bac Giang Province, Vietnam, supported by a complete stock preparation system from pulping to refining. The scope covered an LBKP high-consistency refining line, an NBKP low-consistency refining line and a tissue trimmings line, built around PrimeRefine HC T refining technology that improves short-fiber development, softness and strength. Xuong Giang supplies both tissue jumbo rolls and finished tissue products to domestic and international markets.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: New tissue parent-roll capacity in Vietnam adds regional supply options, so converters should requalify jumbo-roll widths, grades and delivery terms while confirming their slitting and rewinding setup handles the changing furnish mix.

Source: ANDRITZ on Xuong Giang TM16 startup

2Tissue Converting InvestmentAugust 13, 2026

Pehart Tec Grup Adds LC7 Tissue Converting Line at Petresti, Romania

Sebes Info reported Pehart Tec Grup plans a EUR 7.275 million tissue converting line, LC7, at its Petresti mill, adding about 55 tonnes per day, or roughly 18,700 tonnes per year, of finished tissue capacity and lifting total converting capacity by about 47.8 percent. The line includes unwinding, embossing, laminating, rewinding, perforating, adhesive application and an orbital knife, plus automated packaging, case packing, palletizing and wrapping, with some process steps rated up to 600 meters per minute.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: This is a direct roll-to-finished-product converting expansion for bath tissue and kitchen towels, so machinery buyers should benchmark line speed, automated finishing and changeover design when planning similar projects.

Source: Sebes Info on Pehart LC7 converting line

3Tissue Converting CapacityAugust 14, 2026

KP Tissue's Memphis Converting Line Runs Ahead of Plan

On its Q2 earnings call, KP Tissue said the new converting line in Memphis began operating early in the second quarter and is progressing ahead of plan, expanding U.S. capacity for a range of tissue products while the site's broader turnaround meets safety, productivity and sustainability targets. Management is also evaluating smaller investments to add ultra-premium tissue capacity at the facility. Second-quarter revenue rose 2.8 percent to CAD 550.9 million and adjusted EBITDA increased 25 percent to CAD 90.6 million, helped by stronger U.S. volumes.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Faster-than-expected U.S. tissue converting capacity changes regional roll supply and competition, so converters should watch grade availability and keep finishing equipment flexible for premium tissue formats.

Source: Sharewise on KP Tissue Q2 earnings call

4Packaging Paper PricingAugust 10, 2026

AICC Opposes Third Containerboard Price Increase in Five Months

AICC, which represents independent box makers, said the largest containerboard producers announced three price increases in five months, most recently PCA's $140 per ton increase for liner and medium, Smurfit Westrock's $100 per ton increase and International Paper's $80 per ton increase, all effective September 1, 2026. The association argues the increases lack economic justification: earlier $50 per ton increases effective March 1 and June 1 are not fully absorbed, recovered-paper costs have barely moved, and box demand remains near 2016 levels.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Containerboard price actions pass directly into roll material costs and finished-box pricing, so converters should renegotiate near-term supply contracts and keep inventory timing flexible while producers test the market.

Source: AICC on containerboard price increases

5Fiber Packaging Pricing & DemandAugust 12, 2026

Fiber Packaging Q2 Earnings: Price Actions Dominate Producer Calls

Midland reported pricing dominated Q2 fiber packaging earnings calls: Clearwater Paper announced its second $60 per ton price increase of 2026 after shipments rose 8 percent year over year, while Huhtamaki's North American net sales fell nearly 10 percent despite global fiber packaging net sales up 6.7 percent and flexible packaging net sales up nearly 11 percent. Mondi's first-half corrugated packaging revenue rose 4.4 percent to EUR 1.98 billion while its flexible packaging revenue declined 1.1 percent to just over EUR 2 billion, and Sylvamo posted a wider Q2 net loss as it launched a lean transformation.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Diverging demand across corrugated, fiber packaging and flexible film segments helps converters decide where to add capacity and how to price roll material through the third quarter.

Source: Midland on Q2 fiber packaging earnings

1Hygiene Plant InvestmentAugust 4, 2026

Softcare Liberia Signs US$32 Million Hygiene Manufacturing Deal in Monrovia Free Zone

The Liberia Special Economic Zones Authority signed a US$32 million, 25-year Developer and Operator Licensing Agreement with Softcare Liberia to establish a hygiene manufacturing hub in the Monrovia Free Zone. The facility will produce baby diapers, baby wipes, sanitary pads and other hygiene products for the Liberian market and for export across Africa, with more than 150 direct and over 200 indirect jobs expected.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: New diaper and wipes converting capacity in West Africa signals growing regional demand and local-supply competition, so machinery buyers evaluating hygiene lines should compare output, automation and changeover flexibility for multi-product plants.

Source: Knewsonline on Softcare Liberia investment

2Wetlaid Spunlace LineAugust 13, 2026

Zhengzhou Textile Machinery Starts Up CPC Wetlaid Spunlace Line in Turkey

China Textile News reported Zhengzhou Textile Machinery completed installation and commissioning of a CPC (carding plus wetlaid plus carding) wetlaid spunlace line for a well-known Turkish customer and achieved production start-up within one week. The line's products meet EU market access standards and cover bio-based disposable products and premium wiping series, building on the ZFJ and AUTEFA technology cooperation.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: New wetlaid spunlace roll capacity for premium and bio-based wipes changes substrate supply, so wipes converters should verify dispersibility, tensile and winding behavior of new roll goods before high-speed slitting and rewinding.

Source: China Textile News on Zhengzhou CPC spunlace line

3Wipes Quality ComplianceAugust 6, 2026

UK Recalls FreshWipes Intimate Unscented Wipes Over Preservative Failure

The UK Office for Product Safety and Standards recalled FreshWipes Intimate Unscented Wipes in batches LOT 0926 and 0515 because the preservative system did not perform as required, leaving affected batches at risk of microbiological contamination and adverse skin reactions. The wipes, made in China and sold on the FreshWipes website, Amazon UK, TikTok Shop, QVC and other outlets, do not meet Regulation (EC) 1223/2009.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Preservative-system failures can force full batch recalls, so wipes converters should tighten formulation verification, incoming-substrate checks and compliance documentation for EU cosmetics rules.

Source: GOV.UK on FreshWipes recall

4Hygiene Technology & RegulationAugust 11, 2026

INDA Announces Hygienix 2026 Program Focused on Absorbent Core and Manufacturing Advances

INDA published the conference program for Hygienix 2026, to be held November 16-19 in Houston, Texas, covering absorbent cores, superabsorbents, polymers, nonwovens, adhesives, fluid management and skin health alongside sustainability, manufacturing efficiency and evolving regulation. Innovation Award nominations close September 14.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: The program's focus on core materials, fluid management and manufacturing efficiency shows where hygiene converting lines must adapt, so machine buyers should track these signals when specifying dosing, inspection and packaging automation.

Source: INDA on Hygienix 2026 program

5Wipes Contract ManufacturingJuly 6, 2026

GraVo Acquires WAREG and COOLIKE to Expand Wipes Contract Manufacturing

GraVo Beteiligungsgesellschaft acquired German manufacturers WAREG Verpackungs and COOLIKE-Regnery, adding contract filling and packaging for cosmetics, personal care and hygiene plus wet wipes for travel, hospitality and personal care. The group says customers can now source product development, manufacturing, filling and packaging from a single supplier, with formats including sachets, flow packs and wet wipes.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Consolidation in contract wipes manufacturing increases multi-format converting demand, so converters should assess how easily their slitting, rewinding and packaging lines switch between sachets, flow packs and wet-wipe formats.

Source: Onboard Hospitality on GraVo acquisitions

1Recyclable Flexible PackagingAugust 6, 2026

Mondi Wins German Packaging Award for Laser-Marked Recyclable Flexible Packaging

Mondi's concept combining a corrugated outer box with a recyclable, laser-marked flexible packaging format won the German Packaging Award 2026 in the Sustainability category. Laser marking applies artwork during converting without printing plates, enabling faster artwork changes and personalization, and can be applied across formats from reels to pre-made pouches such as the re/cycle StripPouch and FlexiBag, with bio-based feedstocks or post-consumer recycled content options.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Converting-stage laser marking shortens artwork lead times and simplifies film structures, so converters should evaluate whether inline marking and slitting lines can support personalization without slowing throughput.

Source: WhatTheyThink on Mondi laser-marked packaging

2PCR Stretch FilmAugust 3, 2026

GreenDot and DUO PLAST Win Award for Stretch Film with Household PCR

GreenDot and DUO PLAST received the German Packaging Award 2026 in the Logistics and Material Flow category for DUO EARTH YELLOW BACK stretch film, the first to contain up to 10 percent PCR from the German Yellow Bag and Yellow Bin household collection, for 35 percent total recycled content. It works as hand and machine stretch film on existing packaging equipment and helps companies prepare for PPWR minimum recycled-content targets and eco-modulation schemes.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: PCR stretch films made from household recyclate introduce new runnability variables, so film converters should test elongation, recovery force and tension profiles before switching machine stretch lines.

Source: GreenDot on DUO EARTH YELLOW BACK award

3Flexible Packaging CapacityAugust 3, 2026

UFlex Commissions First Overseas WPP Bags Plant in Altamira, Mexico

The Packman reported UFlex commissioned its first overseas woven polypropylene bag plant in Altamira, Mexico, an investment of about USD 52 million with annual capacity of 80 million bags. The facility runs European machinery, operates an integrated converting process with water-based inks at speeds up to 300 meters per minute, and includes in-house recycling, zero solid waste discharge and an integrated quality and environmental control laboratory.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Regional woven bag capacity serving pet-food and industrial packaging demand means converters should confirm their slitting, sealing and handling equipment can process heavier WPP fabrics and frequent bag-size changeovers.

Source: The Packman on UFlex Mexico WPP plant

4PCR Traceability ComplianceJuly 16, 2026

Amcor Earns RecyClass Traceability Certification for Bontite Sustane Stretch Films

Amcor achieved RecyClass Recycled Plastics Traceability Certification for selected Bontite Sustane stretch film specifications produced in Leominster, UK and Zele, Belgium. The blown LLDPE films contain defined levels of post-consumer recycled content and are positioned to support the UK Plastic Packaging Tax change from April 2027, when only post-consumer recycled content will count toward the 30 percent threshold.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Independently verified PCR claims are becoming purchasing requirements, so film converters should keep traceability documentation ready and qualify certified PCR stretch grades for holding force and stretch performance.

Source: Amcor on RecyClass stretch film certification

5Film Recycling InfrastructureAugust 13, 2026

Endolys Wins Approval for £125 Million Plastic Film Recycling Facility in Darlington

Interplas Insights reported Endolys secured planning approval for a £125 million advanced recycling facility on the former Cleveland Bridge site in Darlington, one of the UK's largest projects of its kind, with up to 120 skilled jobs. The plant will install 12 pyrolysis units, with £60 million funded for Phase One's first six units and operations expected to begin before the end of 2026; at full scale it will process up to 120,000 tonnes of plastic film per year into around 80,000 tonnes of recycled pyrolysis oil.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: New flexible-film recycling capacity expands future PCR feedstock for film converters, but output quality and runnability should be requalified before switching grades on slitting and rewinding lines.

Source: Interplas Insights on Endolys Darlington facility
