Table of Contents

  • Why It Matters
  • Buyer Takeaway
  • Sources

What Happened

Major North American containerboard producers announced price increases scheduled for June 2026. The move follows capacity closures and reports of higher input costs.

Why It Matters

Containerboard pricing affects corrugated packaging costs across manufacturing supply chains. It also signals how paper packaging markets are balancing capacity reductions, demand changes and inflation.

Buyer Takeaway

Machinery exporters and converters should monitor paper and corrugated packaging costs because they influence export packing, carton procurement and landed-cost calculations.
