What Happened

Dalian Ruiguang is expanding flushable wipes capacity with a new ANDRITZ line. The added capacity is expected to support production of pulp-based Wetlace nonwovens.

Why It Matters

Flushable wipes growth is tied to biodegradable substrates, dispersibility and regulatory expectations. Pulp-based nonwovens continue to attract investment where brands need more sustainable hygiene products.

Buyer Takeaway

Flushable and dispersible substrates require careful converting. Producers should confirm web strength, water response, perforation behavior and rewinding stability during equipment selection.
