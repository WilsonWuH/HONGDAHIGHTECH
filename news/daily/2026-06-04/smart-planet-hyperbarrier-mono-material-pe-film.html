What Happened

Smart Planet Technologies announced HyperBarrier PE film, a recyclable mono-material PE structure using nanoclay nanocomposite technology. The company says cast film availability is expected by August 2026.

Why It Matters

High-barrier recyclable PE films target applications that traditionally used more complex laminates. Barrier performance, moisture resistance and sealing remain key requirements for food and flexible packaging.

Buyer Takeaway

Converters should watch high-barrier mono-PE developments closely. New films may require adjusted tension control, sealing windows and slitting settings compared with standard PE film.
