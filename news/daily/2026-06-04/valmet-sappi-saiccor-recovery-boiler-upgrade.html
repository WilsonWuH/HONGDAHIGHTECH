What Happened

Valmet was selected by Sappi Southern Africa to deliver an EPC package for recovery boiler component replacement at the Saiccor Mill in South Africa.

Why It Matters

Recovery boiler reliability is central to pulp mill energy and chemical recovery operations. Maintenance and component replacement projects show how paper and pulp producers continue investing in plant reliability.

Buyer Takeaway

For downstream converters, upstream mill reliability can affect pulp and paper availability. Equipment planning should consider supplier stability, raw material flow and maintenance cycles in the wider supply chain.
