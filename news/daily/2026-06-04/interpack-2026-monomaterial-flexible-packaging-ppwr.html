What Happened

At Interpack 2026, suppliers presented mono-material flexible packaging structures designed to meet recyclability expectations under the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation.

Why It Matters

The market is moving along PP and PE material paths. Brand owners and converters are evaluating structures that replace multi-layer laminates while preserving barrier, heat resistance and processing performance.

Buyer Takeaway

Equipment buyers should expect more projects involving mono-material films. Slitting, rewinding and tension systems need to handle thinner films and new layer structures without damaging roll quality.
