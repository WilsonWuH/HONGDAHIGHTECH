What Happened

Woolchemy and BCNonwovens announced a collaboration to scale wool-based hygiene nonwovens. The platform uses hygiene-grade wool and plant-based fibers for moisture management and comfort.

Why It Matters

Disposable hygiene producers are looking for materials that combine performance with renewable or biodegradable positioning. Wool-based nonwovens may be processed through established spunlace routes.

Buyer Takeaway

Material innovation can create new converting requirements. Buyers should verify roll width, web behavior, moisture profile and line compatibility before moving new hygiene substrates into production.
