What Happened

Mondi launched re/loop FlowWrap, a form-fill-and-seal packaging solution for wet wipes. The pack is based on mono-polypropylene and contains 35% post-consumer recycled content.

Why It Matters

Wet wipes packaging often needs barrier performance, sealability and reliable machine handling. Moving from mixed laminates toward mono-material structures is important for brands preparing for recycling rules and customer sustainability targets.

Buyer Takeaway

Converters and hygiene producers should expect more RFQs asking whether wet wipes packaging can support recyclable mono-material formats without sacrificing line speed, seal quality or shelf-life requirements.
