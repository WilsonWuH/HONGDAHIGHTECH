Table of Contents

  • Why It Matters
  • Buyer Takeaway
  • Sources

What Happened

Kruger announced a C$333 million investment to launch a biodegradable nonwoven materials production facility in Trois-Rivieres, Quebec. Operations are expected to begin in 2028.

Why It Matters

The facility is expected to produce biodegradable nonwoven materials mainly for the wipes market. The project links natural fiber use with new manufacturing opportunities in a forestry region.

Buyer Takeaway

Wipes and hygiene producers are expanding interest in plastic-free and natural-fiber substrates. Converters should prepare for more substrate diversity and more testing before scale production.
