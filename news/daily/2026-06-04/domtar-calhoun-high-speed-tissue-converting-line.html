Domtar Starts Installing High-Speed Tissue Converting Line in Tennessee

By HDPTH News | June 4, 2026

Contents

  • What happened
  • Site scope
  • Why it matters
  • Sources
Send Requirements

What happened

Domtar said on February 16, 2026 that its Calhoun, Tennessee site began installing a new high-speed tissue converting line. The company said the project is intended to raise output, improve efficiency, and support future scale.

Site scope

Domtar also said it is expanding warehouse space for parent tissue rolls at Calhoun. The project is meant to align tissue production capacity more closely with converting capability at the site.

Why it matters

For tissue converters, this is a clear example of North American producers investing in downstream finishing rather than only base-sheet capacity. It also points to continued pressure to improve service levels and operating flexibility in the US tissue market.
