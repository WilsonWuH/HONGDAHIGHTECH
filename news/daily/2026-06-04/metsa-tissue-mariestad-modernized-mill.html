What Happened

Metsa Group inaugurated its expanded and modernized tissue paper mill in Mariestad, Sweden. The investment totals EUR 370 million and doubles annual capacity to about 145,000 tonnes.

Why It Matters

The project adds a new tissue machine and three converting lines. It strengthens local tissue supply in Scandinavia and shows continued investment in hygiene paper production despite cost pressure in the wider paper sector.

Buyer Takeaway

Modern tissue and hygiene investments often include both base paper production and downstream converting lines. Equipment buyers should evaluate capacity, energy efficiency and converting flexibility together.
