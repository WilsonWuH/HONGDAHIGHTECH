What Happened

California packaging policy deadlines are increasing pressure on flexible film recycling systems. Industry speakers cited the need for scaled collection, sorting and processing capacity.

Why It Matters

Flexible PE film has value in packaging, but recycling performance depends on collection and contamination control. Policy changes can quickly reshape demand for mono-material films and recyclable structures.

Buyer Takeaway

Flexible packaging buyers should track regional recycling rules when selecting PE film, PP film, laminates and pouch formats. Compliance pressure is becoming a practical purchasing factor, not only a marketing claim.
