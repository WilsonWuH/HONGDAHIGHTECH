1Recovered PaperAugust 21, 2026

OCC Demand Supports Higher August PRN Confidence

Letsrecycle reported that OCC prices rose again in August, supported by demand from Southeast Asia and India, while mixed paper and multigrade also moved higher. The article noted that the European summer usually softens demand, making the current support notable.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Recovered-fibre conditions can affect board and paper economics without changing the machine itself. Converters should keep substrate and core assumptions current and avoid using one historical roll-quality baseline for every supplier.

Source: Letsrecycle August market analysis

2Pulp SupplyAugust 20, 2026

Howe Sound Idle Removes a Significant Pulp Volume from the Market

Domtar said the indefinite idling of Howe Sound and Bayview Fibre in British Columbia will remove about 380,000 metric tonnes of northern bleached softwood kraft pulp from annual supply. The company cited demand, pricing and fibre-availability pressures.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: If a pulp or paper supplier changes, qualify the replacement material with actual parent rolls. Check moisture, thickness, dust, edge strength, tension response and finished-roll handling before a production switch.

Source: Nip Impressions report carrying the Domtar release

3Packaging PaperAugust 19, 2026

AF&PA Sees Packaging Paper Firmness Despite Mixed Grade Signals

AF&PA's July data showed packaging-paper and specialty-packaging shipments up 1% year over year and 5% for the first seven months, while the unbleached operating rate reached 87.5%. The report also showed that food-wrapping shipments were down year over year for July.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: A mixed market makes grade-specific forecasting more valuable. Include the complete paper range, roll diameter, core and finished-width distribution in a slitter-rewinder capacity model.

Source: AF&PA July packaging-paper report

4Printing PaperAugust 20, 2026

AF&PA Reports a Softer July for Printing-Writing Paper

AF&PA's July printing-writing report showed shipments down 9% from July 2025 and inventories up 3% from June. The result reinforces the split between packaging grades and graphic-paper demand.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Plants serving both paper markets should validate the narrowest slit, dust-control and roll-hardness cases separately. A line that handles packaging paper well may still need different knives, extraction or tension recipes for graphic grades.

Source: AF&PA paper statistics page

5Cartonboard NetworkAugust 17, 2026

Arnsberg Deal Highlights Supply-Security and Flexibility Goals

MM Board & Paper said the proposed Arnsberg acquisition should improve production flexibility and supply security within its German platform. The recycled-cartonboard site is expected to remain part of the European board network after approvals and closing.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: When a network changes, downstream buyers should ask whether grade names, roll dimensions, delivery points or quality certificates will change, then schedule a documented first-roll comparison.

Source: MM Group press release on Arnsberg

1Nonwoven RegulationAugust 21, 2026

Nonwoven Manufacturers Face a Moving Legislative Landscape

International Fiber Journal described a complex policy environment for nonwovens, including state packaging rules, recycling definitions, product restrictions and chemical requirements. The article argues that industry participation is needed so hygiene and medical performance are represented in implementation work.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Regulatory uncertainty increases the value of lot traceability and change control. A converting line should make it easy to link the web roll, recipe, operator and inspection result to the customer order.

Source: International Fiber Journal legislative analysis

2PP Nonwoven CostsAugust 21, 2026

PP Spunbond Market Notes Point to Cost Support and Weak Demand

A current PP spunbond market overview reported firm PP raw-material pricing while downstream nonwoven demand remained weak. The article described a market balancing cost support, operating-rate pressure and cautious buying.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: When material cost changes but demand is uncertain, line utilisation and changeover time matter as much as nominal speed. Buyers should compare roll yield, trim, setup time and quality loss in the operating-cost model.

Source: PP spunbond market overview

3Hygiene StandardsJune 8, 2026

INDA's Hygienix Programme Keeps Hygiene Materials and Standards in View

INDA's Hygienix programme covers absorbent hygiene materials, manufacturing, sustainability, regulatory developments and next-generation product technologies. The event profile shows how material and compliance decisions are being discussed together by the hygiene supply chain.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: A hygiene converting RFQ should distinguish general industrial nonwoven capability from the cleanliness, documentation and change-control expectations of regulated or skin-contact products.

Source: INDA latest-news listing for Hygienix

4Fibre InvestmentAugust 19, 2026

Spinnova Listing Plans Keep Alternative Fibre Scale-Up in Focus

Ecotextile News reported that Spinnova is planning a U.S. listing to support its fibre platform. The story is another reminder that alternative fibres continue to move from development toward industrial scale, with financing and process repeatability still central.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: New fibre webs should be treated as new process recipes. Confirm elongation, lint, moisture response, slit edge and roll compression before allowing the grade to run on an automatic production schedule.

Source: Ecotextile News archive

5Cleanroom WipesAugust 19, 2026

Cleanroom Wipes Show Why Substrate and Handling Cannot Be Separated

Cleaning Hygiene Today described cleanroom wipes as part of the contamination-control process, not merely a disposable product. The application connects substrate selection with controlled handling and the condition of the space where the wipe is used.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: For nonwoven roll converters serving cleanroom or medical customers, specify packaging, edge cleanliness, roll identification, inspection and operator controls alongside slit width and speed.

Source: Cleaning Hygiene Today cleanroom-wipe coverage

1Flexible CollectionAugust 21, 2026

Valpak Models a Large Increase in Flexible-Plastic Collection by 2030

Letsrecycle reported Valpak modelling that flexible plastics collected for recycling in England could rise substantially when mandatory household collections are introduced. The analysis estimates that current collection levels are a small share of the flexible-plastic waste stream.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: More collection only creates value if contamination and identification are controlled. Film converters should plan scrap segregation, clear roll labels and a data trail that distinguishes production trim from mixed post-consumer material.

Source: Letsrecycle flexible-plastics collection analysis

2U.S. Plastics PactAugust 21, 2026

EPS Transport Packaging Is Removed from the USPP Problematic-Materials List

Resource Recycling reported that the U.S. Plastics Pact removed EPS transport packaging from its list of problematic and unnecessary materials after evidence of a recycling rate above the programme threshold. The change applies to transport packaging, not to EPS in general.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Material-policy changes should not be translated directly into an equipment change. Buyers still need the exact polymer, thickness, width, end use, collection route and accepted quality criteria for the product being converted.

Source: Resource Recycling on the USPP list revision

3EPR PolicyAugust 21, 2026

California EPR Litigation Adds Uncertainty for Flexible-Packaging Plans

The Flexible Packaging Association described a developing legal challenge involving California extended-producer-responsibility and source-reduction requirements, alongside a pending Oregon ruling. The association said the wider policy environment remains connected across several states.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: For North American film buyers, compliance assumptions should be written with jurisdiction and effective date. Keep material declarations, recycled-content evidence and production records easy to retrieve when customers ask for them.

Source: Flexible Packaging Association policy update

4Recycled Film ProcessingAugust 21, 2026

Huhtamaki Elif Uses Continuous Melt Filtration to Support Recycled Feedstock

Nordson reported that Huhtamaki's Elif division implemented continuous melt-filtration technology in blown-film production to support recycled material use and avoid manual filter changes during operation. The project is positioned as a way to reduce downtime and improve process control.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: More variable recycled feedstock makes downstream roll quality more important. Slitter-rewinder buyers should ask how incoming film variation, edge trim, winding density and defect inspection will be managed in the final roll process.

Source: Nordson technology report on Huhtamaki Elif

5BOPP CapacityAugust 13, 2026

Innovia Highlights Ongoing Investment in North American Labels

Innovia Films' news page describes ongoing investment intended to support North American labels and sustainable label-film solutions. The company supplies coated and high-performance films for packaging, labels and graphics.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Regional film capacity can change lead times and the mix of available grades. A buyer should confirm film width, coating, roll diameter, core and edge requirements before using a new source on an existing slitter.

Source: Innovia Films news page
