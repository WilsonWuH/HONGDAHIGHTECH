Paper, Nonwoven and Film News for August 2, 2026

Fifteen practical stories selected for overseas buyers tracking packaging EPR, tissue energy, recycled paperboard supply, hygiene and medical nonwovens, PPWR compliance, recycled-content film and mono-PE qualification.

HDPTH Daily Market BriefingPaper | Nonwoven | Film

Today's briefing is about implementation risk rather than broad market noise: paper producers are managing EPR, energy, recycled board and pulp supply pressure; nonwoven suppliers are moving hygiene, wipes and medical materials toward higher-function formats; and film converters face PPWR, EPR, PCR and mono-PE qualification demands. 1Paper Packaging RegulationJuly 30, 2026

Oregon EPR Trial Tests Packaging Compliance in Practice

Paper Advance carried PPEC analysis of Oregon's packaging EPR trial, noting that producer responsibility is moving from legislation into operational questions about data, fees, collection, recycling markets and cross-state obligations.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Packaging EPR turns material choice into an operating issue, so converters need traceable rolls, documented waste streams and equipment that keeps recycled or fibre-based packaging output consistent.

Source: Paper Advance/PPEC on packaging EPR implementation

2Tissue Mill EnergyJuly 30, 2026

Toscotec Upgrades Cogeneration at Italian Tissue Mill

Paper Advance listed Toscotec's cogeneration upgrade at Cartiera del Vignaletto in Italy, a tissue-mill energy project aimed at improving production efficiency and reliability.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Energy projects at tissue mills often come with throughput and reliability targets, which push downstream converting lines to remove roll-handling and rewinding bottlenecks.

Source: Paper Advance/Toscotec on Cartiera del Vignaletto cogeneration

3Pulp and Paper SupplyJuly 30, 2026

Canada Announces $100M Loan to Millar Western

Paper Advance reported Canada announced a $100 million loan to Millar Western to support operations, protect jobs and address trade-related uncertainty affecting the pulp and paper sector.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Supply-risk support for pulp and paper producers matters to converters because parent-roll availability, grade substitutions and lead times can change quickly when trade pressure rises.

Source: Paper Advance on Canada loan to Millar Western

4Recycled PaperboardJuly 29, 2026

Clearwater Paper Launches Circa Recycled Paperboard

Paper Advance reported Clearwater Paper launched Circa recycled paperboard with Greenpaper, expanding recycled paperboard supply for U.S. converters.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Recycled paperboard grades can vary in stiffness, dust and caliper, making web guiding, slitting cleanliness and rewind density important when converters qualify new board supply.

Source: Paper Advance on Clearwater Paper Circa CRB

5Paper Packaging AutomationJuly 28, 2026

Packaging Industry Shifts from Strategy to Implementation

Paper Advance said fibre-based packaging producers are moving from strategy to practical implementation of PPWR, AI and automation across production.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Implementation pressure favors machinery that can produce repeatable roll quality, capture process data and support faster setup as packaging formats and compliance checks change.

Source: Paper Advance on packaging implementation pressure

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: A stronger cellulosic fibre focus can change softness, linting and elongation behavior, so buyers should test unwinding, perforation and slitting before scaling new wipe substrates.

1Packaging ComplianceJuly 31, 2026

France Delays Business Packaging EPR Until 2027

Packaging Insights reported France postponed its industrial and commercial packaging EPR scheme from July 1, 2026 to January 1, 2027 because companies lacked clarity on obligations, fees and registration.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: EPR delays do not remove compliance pressure; converters still need material traceability, waste data and roll documentation ready when customers restart qualification work.

Source: Packaging Insights on France business packaging EPR delay

2Film RegulationJuly 31, 2026

PPWR, SB 54 and UK Collections Drive Flexible-Packaging Reviews

Packaging Insights said July brought preparation for core EU PPWR obligations on August 12, challenges around California SB 54, China delivery-packaging rules and the UK delay to flexible plastic collections in England.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Regulation is pushing brands to audit film structures, recycled content and reporting methods, so converters should align equipment choices with documentation and repeatable quality control.

Source: Packaging Insights July packaging regulation review

3Mono-PE Film StructureJuly 29, 2026

ExxonMobil and Bandera Develop Recyclable PE Pet Food Pouch

PlasticsToday reported ExxonMobil Signature Polymers and Bandera developed a recyclable PE pet food pouch using conventional blown film extrusion, targeting stiffness, optics, dimensional stability, printing and lamination requirements without MDO equipment.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Mono-PE structures can still be demanding to convert, so buyers should validate COF, heat response, slit quality and rewind profile before replacing PET-based laminates.

Source: PlasticsToday on ExxonMobil and Bandera PE pet food pouch

4PCR Film PackagingJuly 28, 2026

IPG Launches Air Pillow Film with 50% Recycled Content

Eco-plastics in Packaging listed IPG's air pillow film with 50% recycled content, including 35% third-party certified post-consumer recycled material and 15% post-industrial recycled material.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Higher-PCR films can vary in gauge, seal behavior and puncture resistance, so converters need stable tension, inspection and winding control to keep protective packaging consistent.

Source: Eco-plastics in Packaging on IPG recycled-content air pillow film

5Film Recycling SupplyJuly 20, 2026

Aliplast Buys Kronos Stake to Expand Recycled Polymer Capacity

Packaging Insights reported Aliplast gained a majority stake in Kronos Polymer Polska, expanding recycling activity involving PET, LDPE, HDPE and PP materials used in packaging supply chains.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: More recycled polymer supply supports PCR film adoption, but converters still need machinery settings that handle recycled-resin variability without wrinkles, breaks or poor roll formation.

Source: Packaging Insights on Aliplast and Kronos Polymer Polska
