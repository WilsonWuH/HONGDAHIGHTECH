1Recycled-Paper CostJuly 2, 2026

Koehler Raises Recycled-Paper Prices by 6%

Koehler Paper will increase recycled-paper prices by 6% for deliveries from September 1. The company cited higher raw-material, energy, freight and logistics costs, despite internal efficiency measures.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: A known September increase gives plants time to reduce trim, setup scrap and rejected rolls; precise slitting and repeatable tension settings become direct cost-control tools.

Source: PaperAge on Koehler's recycled-paper price increase

2Pulp Supply PlanningJuly 2, 2026

Mercer Extends Rosenthal Pulp Shutdown to One Month

Mercer International extended the planned maintenance shutdown at its Rosenthal mill in Germany from two weeks to the full month of September. The mill produces about 360,000 tonnes per year of NBSK kraft pulp for paper and tissue markets.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: A longer outage can tighten regional pulp availability or alter parent-reel costs, so converters should confirm safety stock and preserve flexibility across tissue grades and reel specifications.

Source: PaperAge on the Mercer Rosenthal shutdown

3Packaging-Paper DemandJune 25, 2026

Packaging-Paper Shipments Rise 12% in the United States

AF&PA reported that U.S. packaging-paper and specialty-packaging shipments in May were 12% above May 2025 and 6% higher for the first five months of 2026. Bleached food-wrapping shipments increased 5.6% year over year.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Stronger shipment growth supports capacity planning, but the 89% unbleached-paper operating rate means investment should target bottlenecks and saleable throughput rather than headline machine speed alone.

Source: PaperAge on AF&PA's May shipment data

4Converting Network ResetJune 26, 2026

International Paper Consolidates Four Converting Sites

International Paper plans to stop preprint operations in Richwood, Kentucky, and close a sheet plant in Aurora, Illinois, plus converting plants in Elk Grove, California, and Barrington, New Jersey by the end of the third quarter. Customers will be moved to other regional facilities.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Network consolidation transfers volume and product mix to fewer plants, increasing the value of fast changeovers, dependable uptime and material-handling capacity at the receiving sites.

Source: PaperAge on International Paper's site consolidation

5Food-Packaging MaterialsJuly 1, 2026

Fibre-Based Food Packaging Share Is Forecast to Reach 42%

A UPM Specialty Materials and Smithers survey of more than 230 packaging professionals projects fibre-based packaging's share of the global food-packaging market rising from 37% today to 42% by 2045. Respondents identified barrier coatings and regulation as major drivers.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: More coated and laminated paper structures will require controlled web tension, surface protection and clean edges, especially when plants switch between paper and film-based jobs.

Source: PaperAge on the UPM and Smithers study

1U.S. Hygiene CapacityJuly 1, 2026

DiloGroup Wins U.S. Hygiene Needling-Line Order

DiloGroup said it received an order for a complete U.S. needling line for hygiene materials. The scope covers fiber opening and filtration, card feeding, carding, crosslapping, needling and CV1 web-control technology.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Stable web formation and particle control upstream improve downstream slit quality, but converters should still validate basis-weight variation, edge integrity and winding pressure on the new roll goods.

Source: Textile World on DiloGroup's U.S. hygiene line

2Regional Nonwoven SupplyJune 19, 2026

Supreme Nonwoven Plans First U.S. Factory

Supreme Nonwoven will invest US$25.8 million in a more than 200,000-square-foot facility in Lexington, North Carolina, creating 50 jobs. The plant will supply advanced nonwovens for filtration, automotive and other industrial applications.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Local supply can shorten lead times and enable customized webs, but each new construction still needs disciplined incoming-roll trials and stored recipes for tension, slitting and rewind density.

Source: Textile World on Supreme Nonwoven's U.S. investment

3Diaper ManufacturingJune 7, 2026

Indonesia Diaper Plant Targets 900 Units per Minute

PT Multi Medika Internasional and Fujian Nice Paper are developing a diaper factory in Tangerang, Indonesia, through their joint venture. The planned line is designed for up to 900 diapers per minute, equivalent to about 1.8 billion units annually.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: High-speed diaper output requires synchronized nonwoven, fluff, film and elastic supply, making accurate roll preparation, splice quality and automated roll handling critical to line utilization.

Source: IDNFinancials on the Tangerang diaper plant

4Bio-Based Hygiene WebsJune 2, 2026

Wool-Based Spunlace Moves Toward Industrial Scale

Woolchemy and BCNonwovens agreed to scale Woolchemy's neweFlex spunlace platform after a successful manufacturing pilot and validation work. Target applications include wipes, baby care, feminine hygiene and incontinence products.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Wool-containing webs may behave differently from viscose, pulp or synthetic spunlace, so buyers should test dust, stretch, cut quality, perforation force and roll compression before commercial runs.

Source: BCNonwovens on the Woolchemy scale-up partnership

5Disinfecting WipesJuly 2, 2026

Plant-Based Disinfecting Wipes Expand Mass-Market Choice

Biom's disinfecting wipes received a 2026 HGTV Editors' Clean Home Award. The product combines a citric-acid, quat-free formula with a 100% plant-based biodegradable wipe, adding another differentiated format to the consumer wipes market.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Formula and substrate changes affect wet pickup, fold stability, cutting and pack sealing, so flexible dosing control and hygienic changeovers are essential for multi-product wipes plants.

Source: Biom press release on its plant-based disinfecting wipes

1Mono-PE Refill PouchJune 22, 2026

Magiclean Converts Refill Pouch to Mono-PE

Kao Thailand, SCGP, SCGC and Dow launched a 700 ml Magiclean floor-cleaner refill pouch made as a mono-material PE structure for Thailand's mechanical recycling stream. The commercial pack uses HDPE for print performance and Dow PE resins in the sealant layer.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Replacing mixed laminates with all-PE changes stiffness, heat response and tension windows, so converters need fine web control, wrinkle prevention and repeatable sealing-compatible roll quality.

Source: SCGP on the Magiclean mono-material pouch

2Variable-Data PrintingJune 26, 2026

GS1 Sunrise 2027 Forces Flexible-Packaging Print Upgrades

The global move from traditional 1D barcodes to QR codes and GS1 DataMatrix at retail point of sale is pushing flexible-packaging producers to audit printing, artwork and product-data workflows during 2026. The target transition date is 2027.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Smaller, data-rich codes require stable registration and web handling; slitting accuracy, roll tracking and inspection systems must protect print position and code readability through finishing.

Source: PlasticsToday on the flexible-packaging 2D-code transition

3Recyclable Food PouchJune 18, 2026

Walki and Dow Develop Matte MDO-PE Frozen-Food Pouch

Walki and Dow developed a mono-material MDO-PE laminate with a matte finish for recyclable food pouches, initially targeting frozen foods. The structure is intended to replace conventional oriented-PET print webs while meeting PPWR design requirements.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Oriented PE can be thin and directionally sensitive, making low-tension unwinding, static control, sharp slitting and stable rewind density important qualification criteria.

Source: Packaging Gateway coverage of the Walki-Dow MDO-PE pouch

4Barrier BOPP CapacityMay 20, 2026

Surya Adds Wide AlOx BOPP Metallizing Capacity

Surya Global Flexifilms ordered its fourth BOBST metallizer, a 3,650 mm EXPERT K5 with AlOx capability, for installation at the end of 2026. The line will add clear high-barrier BOPP capacity alongside three existing metallizers.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Wide coated webs create more downstream slit lanes, but coating uniformity, wrinkle control, edge quality and defect isolation must remain stable across the full width.

Source: BOBST on Surya Global Flexifilms' fourth metallizer

5Winding-Core SupplyMarch 16, 2026

Sonoco Expands Precision Plastic-Core Capacity

Sonoco Plastics is investing EUR 850,000 in extrusion and injection-molding capacity at Troisdorf, Germany, to increase production of 3-inch plastic winding cores, endwalls and related parts for film and tape applications.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Core straightness and dimensional tolerance directly affect roll concentricity, vibration and finished-roll quality, especially at high rewind speeds and on narrow slit widths.

Source: Packaging Insights on Sonoco's film-core investment
