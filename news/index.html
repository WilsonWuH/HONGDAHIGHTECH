1Industrial Paper PackagingJuly 27, 2026

Sonoco Says URB Demand and Paper Can Growth Remain Strong

PaperAge reported Sonoco second-quarter results showing North America uncoated recycled board trade tons up 6%, mill utilization at 95%, and projected paper can growth in Europe, Asia and South America. Sonoco also said strong URB demand requires imports from its Europe and Latin America mills through the third quarter.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Strong URB and paper-can demand keeps pressure on core quality, slit width accuracy and rewind consistency as converters run more recycled-board and paper-packaging jobs.

Source: PaperAge on Sonoco second-quarter packaging results

2Packaging Materials QualificationJuly 27, 2026

Billerud Advances North American Packaging Materials Strategy

PaperAge reported Billerud improved second-quarter profitability and said its North American shift toward packaging materials is progressing. The company cited strong interest in locally produced coated liner and planned targeted machine upgrades at Escanaba during a September shutdown.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: New coated liner and packaging grades require converters to validate surface friction, caliper stability, slitting dust, tension recipes and roll hardness before accepting large orders.

Source: PaperAge on Billerud second-quarter results

3Paper Supply-Chain RiskJuly 25, 2026

U.S. Tariffs Target Canadian Pulp, Tissue and Packaging Products

PaperAge carried an AF&PA update saying new U.S. proclamations impose 50% tariffs on a broad range of Canadian goods, including dissolving pulp, tissue and hygiene papers, containerboard, specialty papers, coated paperboard and converted paper products. The tariffs are scheduled for August 19, 2026 unless negotiations change the outcome.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Tariff risk can force converters to qualify substitute rolls quickly, so adjustable tension control, incoming inspection and clean slitting become practical supply-risk tools.

Source: PaperAge on AF&PA tariff warning

4Corrugated DemandJuly 23, 2026

PCA Reports Record Corrugated Shipments and Full Mill Operation

Packaging Corporation of America reported second-quarter net sales of $2.5 billion and said total corrugated products shipments were up 24.3% per day from the year earlier period. The company said it continued operating its mill system at full capacity and reduced export sales to support domestic corrugated demand.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Strong corrugated demand favors converters with reliable high-throughput slitting, trim removal and roll handling that can keep packaging plants supplied without creating edge or winding defects.

Source: PaperAge on PCA second-quarter results

5Paper Machine ConversionJuly 21, 2026

Palm to Convert King's Lynn PM 7 from Newsprint to Corrugated Paper

PaperAge reported that Palm Group will convert PM 7 at its King's Lynn mill in the UK from newsprint to corrugated case material. The project adds another signal that producers are shifting assets toward packaging grades where demand is stronger.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Grade conversions change the parent-roll mix reaching downstream converters, raising the value of flexible slitters and rewinders that can handle different stiffness, basis weight and roll profiles.

Source: PaperAge on Palm PM 7 conversion

1Nonwoven Winding EquipmentJuly 27, 2026

Merkas Tekstil Starts Up A.Celli Winder and Rewinder

Nonwovens Industry reported that Merkas Tekstil started up an A.Celli winder and rewinder for an air-through bonding nonwoven production line in Turkey. The item highlights end-of-line roll formation as a live investment area for nonwoven producers.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: ATB and hygiene nonwovens need controlled roll build, clean edges and repeatable rewind tension before they can feed converting or product-assembly lines reliably.

Source: Nonwovens Industry on Merkas Tekstil A.Celli startup

2Technical Nonwoven RecyclingJuly 6, 2026

A Penteadora Starts Textile Recycling and Needlepunch Lines in Portugal

ANDRITZ said A Penteadora started a complete mechanical textile recycling line and needlepunch nonwoven line in Unhais da Serra, Portugal. The lines process pre- and post-consumer textile waste into materials for thermal insulation, acoustic barriers and protective applications.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Recycled-fiber nonwovens can vary in blend and thickness, so converters need robust unwinding, web guiding, knife setup and inspection to keep technical rolls consistent.

Source: ANDRITZ on A Penteadora recycling and needlepunch lines

3Wipes Product InnovationJuly 14, 2026

Rockline Wins World of Wipes Award for Fentanyl Detection Wipe

Nonwovens Industry reported that the 2026 World of Wipes Innovation Award went to Rockline Industries for its First Defense Fentanyl Detection Wipe. The coverage followed the World of Wipes event in Nashville and shows wipes moving into more specialized safety and detection use cases.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Specialty wipes raise converting expectations for dosing accuracy, substrate cleanliness, perforation quality and lot documentation, especially where end users need consistent functional performance.

Source: Nonwovens Industry on the 2026 World of Wipes award

4Wipes PipelineJune 24, 2026

World of Wipes Finalists Point to Functional and Flushable Substrate Demand

Nonwovens Industry reported finalists for the World of Wipes Innovation Award, including Rockline's First Defense Fentanyl Detection Wipe. The finalist list reflected wipes moving beyond commodity cleaning into functional, safety and application-specific formats.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: More specialized wipe formats create demand for converters that can combine soft-web handling, precise perforation, stable roll density and clean packaging interfaces.

Source: Nonwovens Industry on World of Wipes finalists

5Web Handling SupplyJuly 27, 2026

Maxcess Reorganizes Americas Sales for Web Handling Customers

Nonwovens Industry reported that Maxcess announced a new Americas leadership structure. While organizational, the update matters because Maxcess supplies web handling, guiding, winding and tension-control components used across nonwoven, film and paper converting lines.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Converters planning upgrades should watch web-handling supplier support because local service, controls knowledge and component availability directly affect uptime and retrofit decisions.

Source: Nonwovens Industry on Maxcess Americas structure

1Specialty BOPP FilmJuly 22, 2026

Cosmo Films Launches Antifog Biodegradable BOPP Film

Labels & Labeling reported that Cosmo Films launched a transparent antifog biodegradable film for fresh food packaging. The co-extruded BOPP film has heat-sealable and treated surfaces, hot and cold antifog performance, printability and high seal strength for fresh-cut vegetables, salads, meat and flower wrapping.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Antifog and biodegradable film grades require converters to check seal-side orientation, coating durability, COF, static, slitting edge quality and winding tension before production scale-up.

Source: Labels & Labeling on Cosmo Films antifog biodegradable film

2Flexible Packaging CapacityJuly 20, 2026

Eco Flexibles Opens Northampton Supersite for Recyclable Flexibles

Labels & Labeling reported that Eco Flexibles opened a Northampton, UK supersite with two Fujifilm Jet Press FP790 digital presses and two Karlville pouching machines. The facility supports mono-polymer and paper-based recyclable flexible packaging for food, pet and FMCG brands, with lamination, pouching and slitting capabilities.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: New pouch and slitting capacity confirms that converters are investing in short-run, recyclable flexible packaging where fast changeover, roll traceability and clean slit edges matter.

Source: Labels & Labeling on Eco Flexibles Northampton supersite

3Automated Film ConvertingJuly 20, 2026

Amcor Expands Dongguan Flexible Packaging Plant

Labels & Labeling reported Amcor began expanding its Dongguan, China flexible packaging plant with a 7,000 sqm manufacturing facility and automated warehouse, due for completion by July 2027. The project includes automated solvent-free laminators, high-speed bag-making machines and automated bag arranging systems for recycle-ready food, home and personal care packaging.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Automation and recycle-ready structures push film converters toward more stable parent-roll quality, tighter slitting tolerances and reliable winding for high-speed downstream machines.

Source: Labels & Labeling on Amcor Dongguan expansion

4Recyclable Ink and Adhesive SystemJuly 19, 2026

Sun Chemical Earns RecyClass Certification for Flexible Packaging System

Labels & Labeling reported that Sun Chemical's SunUno SoliCycle inks and SunLam adhesives earned RecyClass certification as a complete recyclable system. The development gives flexible packaging converters another route for qualifying print and lamination choices under recyclability requirements.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Certified ink and adhesive systems still need process validation, because coating weight, lamination quality, web tension and rewind profile affect whether recyclable films perform consistently.

Source: Labels & Labeling on Sun Chemical RecyClass certification

5Flexible Film RecyclingJuly 1, 2026

Packaging Dive Says Film Recycling Is in a Pivotal Proof Year

Packaging Dive reported that speakers at the 2026 Packaging Recycling Summit said the next 12 months are crucial for proving flexible-film recycling models. The report noted California EPR pressure, pilot programs, a low film recycling rate and the need to develop end markets for recycled film material.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: As EPR and PCR demand grow, converters need production records, material separation discipline, clean roll handling and consistent film quality to support recycled-content claims.

Source: Packaging Dive on flexible film recycling progress
