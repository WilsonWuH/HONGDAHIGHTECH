1Fibre Packaging ProcessAugust 17, 2026

IFPC 2026 Agenda Highlights Dry Forming, Recyclability and Food-Contact Fibre

RISE released additional programme details for the International Fibre Moulding and Paper Forming Conference, scheduled for September 23-24 in Stockholm. The agenda covers 3D-shaped fibre products and paper-based packaging through sessions on trends and markets, processes and products, recyclability, and innovation and R&D. Topics include recycled fibre for food-contact applications, dry forming, fibre layering, mineral additives and hot-pressed fibre networks.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Fibre-packaging scale-up will create new sheet and roll converting trials, so buyers should prepare for tighter control of moisture, stiffness, dust and edge quality when qualifying paper-based alternatives.

Source: Paper Advance on IFPC 2026 fibre packaging agenda

2Paper Flexible PackagingAugust 17, 2026

Paper-Based Flexible Packaging Gains Investment but Still Faces Performance Barriers

Paper Advance reported that paper-based flexible packaging is attracting brand and investor interest, but cost, performance, sourcing and end-of-life barriers still limit broad deployment. The article cited Ellen MacArthur Foundation criteria for responsible paper-flexibles transition and noted that most demanding applications still need coatings, barrier layers, adhesives or inks to meet moisture, grease and oxygen protection requirements.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Converters evaluating paper-flexible rollstock need slitting, rewinding and inspection equipment that can handle coated papers without edge dust, curl, delamination or inconsistent roll hardness.

Source: Paper Advance on paper flexible packaging barriers

3Mill FlexibilityAugust 17, 2026

Alberta Supports C$50 Million Whitecourt Paper Mill Expansion

Alberta will provide more than C$1.5 million toward Alberta Newsprint Company's nearly C$50 million Whitecourt mill expansion. The project will modernize the facility and let it manufacture multiple paper grades as the mill diversifies beyond traditional newsprint. The expansion is expected to create 13 permanent jobs, support 25 construction jobs and retain more than 220 existing roles.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: When mills diversify from newsprint into higher-value grades, downstream converters should expect new specifications and should test width, tension and surface behavior before committing capacity.

Source: Paper Advance on Alberta Newsprint Whitecourt expansion

4Fibre Food PackagingAugust 15, 2026

Metsa Invests EUR 25 Million in Muoto Fibre Food-Packaging Line

Metsa Group will invest about EUR 25 million in a commercial production line for Muoto fibre-based food packaging at Aanekoski, Finland. The line is scheduled to start in 2028 with nominal annual capacity above 100 million products, and Valmet will supply the production technology. Muoto packaging is made directly from wet wood pulp using Metsa's own fibres.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Industrialization of moulded and fibre-based food packaging can shift demand from plastic formats to fibre substrates, increasing the need for stable feeding, trimming, roll handling and quality checks around paper-based converting.

Source: Paper Advance on Metsa Muoto food-packaging investment

5Boxboard DemandAugust 6, 2026

AF&PA Reports U.S. Boxboard Production Up 2 Percent in Q2

AF&PA's Q2 2026 Boxboard Quarterly Report said U.S. boxboard production increased 2 percent year over year in the second quarter. The report is a practical signal for carton, foodservice and packaging converters because boxboard demand and production balance influence order timing, grade availability and inventory decisions.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Higher boxboard output supports packaging-converting workload, so machine buyers should review slitter-rewinder throughput, trim handling and finished-roll consistency before bottlenecks appear.

Source: Paper Advance listing AF&PA Q2 2026 boxboard report

1Hygiene Raw MaterialsAugust 18, 2026

Indonesia Opens Anti-Dumping Investigation into Chinese SAP Imports

Nonwovens News reported that Indonesia's Anti-Dumping Committee has opened an investigation into imports of superabsorbent polymers from China after an application by PT Nippon Shokubai Indonesia. The case was announced on August 14 and could affect material sourcing and cost structures for Indonesia's absorbent hygiene supply chain.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: SAP cost or sourcing changes can alter diaper and sanitary product economics, so converters should watch material availability and keep unwind, lamination and inspection setups flexible for alternate absorbent-core specifications.

Source: Nonwovens News on Indonesia SAP dumping investigation

2Absorbent Hygiene DevelopmentAugust 18, 2026

BASF Opens Mumbai SAP and Diaper Performance Lab

BASF opened a Global Performance Lab for diapers and superabsorbent polymers at its Innovation Campus Asia Pacific in Mumbai, India. The facility will provide technical and application services to SAP customers worldwide, focused on absorbent hygiene product development and performance testing.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: More local testing support can speed diaper-core optimization, which means hygiene producers may requalify topsheet, acquisition layer, backsheet and core materials more often.

Source: Nonwovens News on BASF Mumbai SAP and diaper lab

3Hygiene Backsheet MaterialsAugust 14, 2026

Breathable PE Backsheet Film Demand Targets Hygiene Growth

Nonwovens News cited research projecting the global market for breathable PE backsheet films in absorbent hygiene products to rise from US$1.76 billion in 2025 to US$2.56 billion by 2032. Breathable backsheets are used in baby diapers, adult incontinence products, sanitary napkins and related disposable absorbent products to prevent leakage while allowing water vapour transmission.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Thinner and softer breathable backsheets require precise tension, static control and roll-edge quality because film and nonwoven layers must run cleanly through hygiene converting lines.

Source: Nonwovens News on breathable PE films for hygiene

4Medical Nonwoven ApplicationAugust 17, 2026

Calla Lily Joins Wellcome Leap Programme with Nonwoven-Based Drug-Delivery Platform

Calla Lily Clinical Care joined Wellcome Leap's US$50 million Focused Antibiotics programme to assess its Callavid intravaginal drug-delivery platform for urinary tract infection treatment. The London-based company will evaluate whether its leak-free, tampon-like nonwoven-based device can deliver antibiotics locally while limiting systemic exposure.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Medical nonwoven formats raise the bar for clean slitting, web handling, traceability and sterile-packaging compatibility, especially when materials move into regulated drug-delivery applications.

Source: Nonwovens News on Calla Lily nonwoven drug-delivery platform

5Nonwoven Process TechnologyAugust 14, 2026

ANDRITZ to Showcase Needlepunch and Spunlace Technology at CINTE 2026

Nonwovens News reported that ANDRITZ will showcase technology for needlepunch and spunlace nonwovens at CINTE 2026. The coverage highlights the continuing focus on production systems for wipes, hygiene and durable nonwoven applications as producers look for higher efficiency and broader material flexibility.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: New web-forming and bonding technology usually leads to new parent-roll grades, so converters should plan qualification runs for basis weight variation, elongation, lint and roll build before commercial launch.

Source: Nonwovens News on ANDRITZ CINTE 2026 technology

1Regulation & ComplianceAugust 12, 2026

EU PPWR Now Applies as Packaging Industry Moves to Evidence-Based Compliance

Packaging Insights reported that the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation generally started applying on August 12, 2026. The regulation applies to packaging placed on the EU market across materials and sets requirements for manufacturing, composition, reuse and recovery, pushing companies toward recyclable and reusable formats backed by stronger evidence.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Flexible packaging converters need technical files, material declarations and recyclable-structure trials ready, because compliance evidence now influences whether film packs can enter EU supply chains.

Source: Packaging Insights on PPWR implementation

2PCR Stretch FilmAugust 14, 2026

Amcor Gains RecyClass Traceability Certification for PCR Stretch Films

Interplas Insights reported that Amcor achieved RecyClass Recycled Plastics Traceability Certification under EN15343 for selected Bontite Sustane stretch film specifications. The films contain post-consumer recycled content and are positioned to help customers prepare for UK Plastic Packaging Tax changes in 2027.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Certified PCR stretch films make traceability part of rollstock purchasing, so buyers should verify recycled-content documentation together with elongation, puncture resistance and rewind quality.

Source: Interplas Insights on Amcor RecyClass stretch films

3Flexible Film RecyclingAugust 3, 2026

California Film Recycling Pilots Test Commercial Collection Models

Waste Dive reported that Flexible Film Recycling Alliance pilots are targeting more film packaging from warehouses and retail backrooms for recycling. The alliance is also working on a standard to verify recycled film collection, monitor contamination and confirm that collected materials are processed into credible new products, while California's EPR requirements add pressure to scale end markets.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Commercial film recycling depends on clean, traceable film streams, so converters and machine buyers should consider trim recovery, roll identification and contamination control when upgrading slitting and rewinding lines.

Source: Waste Dive on California commercial film recycling pilots

4Mono-Material PE FilmJune 22, 2026

Walki and Dow Develop Recyclable Matte MDO-PE Pouch

PlasticsToday reported that Walki and Dow developed a recyclable mono-material MDO-PE laminate with a premium matte finish for frozen, dry and fresh food pouches. The structure uses MDO-PE as the print web and a PE-based sealant layer, with low-temperature sealing technology to protect the heat-sensitive oriented film during pouch making.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: MDO-PE pouches can replace PET/PE laminates but require careful control of heat, tension, shrinkage and winding, so converters should test roll handling before scaling mono-material PE structures.

Source: PlasticsToday on Walki and Dow recyclable MDO-PE pouch

5Recycled BOPP FilmJune 11, 2026

Innovia and PureCycle Produce White Cavitated BOPP Film with 40 Percent Recycled PP

Packaging Europe reported that Innovia Films produced white cavitated BOPP film using PureCycle recycled polypropylene resin, unlocking more than 40 percent post-consumer recycled content. The film is targeted at conventional food-contact and flexible-packaging uses where brands want recycled content without moving away from BOPP performance.

Why it matters for converters and machine buyers: Recycled-content BOPP gives snack and label converters a compliance path, but they should requalify haze, stiffness, print registration, slit edge quality and winding density against virgin grades.

Source: Packaging Europe on Innovia and PureCycle recycled BOPP film
