Nonwoven Drying Oven Systems

Industrial drying systems combine controlled web transport, recirculating hot air and custom production-line integration for nonwoven and flexible materials.

Thermal Process Integration

Configured around material, moisture load and line speed.

Drying capacity cannot be selected from width alone. HDPTH reviews material composition, incoming moisture, target residual moisture, web width, production speed, heat source and available factory space before proposing a system.

  • Custom working width and chamber length
  • Recirculating hot-air and exhaust arrangement by process
  • Web transport designed for stable material handling
  • Electrical, gas or other heat-source requirements reviewed by project
