Как выбрать машину резки нетканых материалов

Before asking for price, buyers should confirm material, roll width, cutting quality, speed target, tension control and downstream production requirements.

Key Selection Factors

  • Material type, GSM or thickness
  • Parent roll width and finished roll width
  • Required speed and production shift plan
  • Knife system and changeover frequency
  • Rewinding method and target roll diameter
  • Available factory space and power requirements

RFQ Checklist

Send HDPTH your material photo, target product, width range, speed expectation and destination country. The sales and technical team can then recommend a suitable machine configuration.
