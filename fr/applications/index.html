Applications des machines non-tissé

HDPTH machines are used by manufacturers that process nonwoven and flexible roll materials for hygiene, medical, disposable, packaging and industrial production.

Application Visual

Nonwoven roll converting for clean production environments.

These visuals support hygiene, medical and disposable product buyers who need to understand material flow, roll output and production cleanliness quickly.

Hygiene Produits

Baby diapers, sanitary napkins and adult incontinence product supply chains that require stable slitting and rewinding.

Medical & Healthcare

Medical nonwoven materials and surgical consumables where clean production and stable roll output matter.

Disposable Produits

Wipes and disposable nonwoven product manufacturers needing perforating, rewinding or combined lines.

Paper & Packaging

Paper and packaging material producers converting wide rolls into production-ready formats.

Film & Textile

Flexible material converters that require precise cutting, roll handling and custom machine layouts.

Industrial Nonwoven

Industrial processing companies with custom material, width, speed and downstream equipment requirements.
