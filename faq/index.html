Nonwoven Converting Machine FAQ

Short, direct answers for buyers comparing slitting, rewinding, perforating and automatic knife equipment.

What does a nonwoven slitting machine do?

It cuts wide nonwoven rolls into narrower rolls with controlled tension, clean edges and accurate rewinding.

What is a nonwoven rewinding machine?

It rewinds roll materials into target roll formats for downstream converting, storage or production use.

Can HDPTH customize machine configuration?

Yes. Width, speed, knife system, winding method, control setup and auxiliary equipment can be discussed by project.

Which industries use HDPTH equipment?

Hygiene products, medical nonwoven materials, disposable products, paper, packaging, film, textile and industrial nonwoven processing.

What information is needed for quotation?

Material type, parent roll width, finished roll width, roll diameter, target speed, application industry and destination country.

Do you provide complete converting solutions?

HDPTH provides slitting, rewinding, perforating, automatic knife systems and auxiliary equipment for integrated production needs.
